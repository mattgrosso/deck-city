# The economy: everything in budget

Generated by `yarn price` from `src/game/core/economy/economy.js` (the values) and `priceList.js` (the pricing). Do not edit by hand; change a value or a card and run it again. `economy.test.js` fails when anything lands off the line without a named bargain.

**The unit is one budget**, one coin of this turn's treasury. A trade is FAIR between 0.75 and 1.4 (what you get over what you pay). Off that line is a mistake unless it is a named **bargain**.

| one of | budget | why |
|---|---|---|
| budget | 1 | the unit |
| revenue | 2.4 | a point every turn for 4 turns, at 0.6 spent (use-it-or-lose-it, five cards to spend it on) |
| population | 1.5 | the win condition; anchored so a lone house lands fair |
| jobs | 0.5 | a job keeps one person out of the unemployment Crime; shared, so half |
| status | -2 | a dead draw about every 2 turns until cleared |
| draw | 1 | one more choice this turn |
| discard | 0.5 | thinning a bad draw |
| buy | 1.5 | one more purchase at the city row this turn: a fair buy's surplus, a turn sooner |
| rollover | 4.8 | about 2 kept a turn, for 4 turns, at 0.6 |

Lasting effects count over **4 turns**; the deck comes round every **2**. A "next door" bonus assumes 1 good neighbour and 0.5 bad one (players chase the first and dodge the second). A facility clears 0.5 status cards a turn. Disasters strike every 4 turns.

The starting deck is worth **10.9** budget over its cost.

## Off the line (0)

Nothing. Every card is fair or a named bargain.

## Bargains (cheap or dear on purpose)

- `play:road`: the city's skeleton: cheap so building never stalls for want of a street
- `play:civic-recycle`: free on purpose: the starter deck's filter, worth only what it digs past
